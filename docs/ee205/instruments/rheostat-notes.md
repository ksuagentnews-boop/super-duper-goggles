# Rheostats (Exp 5): notes (batch 5, item 6)

Sources: lab crops `crops/rheostats-row.jpg`, `crops/rheostat-front.jpg`, `crops/rheostat-slider-hand.jpg`; layout confirmed by
`ref/rheostat-leybold.jpg`. Model: Leybold slide rheostat 537 3x, 330 × 95 × 160 mm (L × W × H); drawn 1 unit ≈ 1 mm.

| Element | Look / place |
|---|---|
| Count | four rheostats side by side (three are the Y load; the fourth stands on the table too, its sockets take any lead) |
| Body (drawn from above) | long beige housing, top split in two halves along the length, rows of ventilation slits along both long sides, two screws at the near end |
| Slider | black carriage astride the gap between the halves, a pale label plate on top; dragged along the rail (R follows), 1 Ω per wheel / pad step |
| End panel (drawn face-on, a separate part) | dark grey plate wrapped by the beige sheet: red 4 mm socket at the top (winding end), round recessed element with a black centre, «max 500 V», black socket (slider) bottom-left, ⏚ mark, green-yellow earth socket bottom-right |
| Left out | the two yellow ⚠ stickers (owner's choice); the old −10 / −1 / +1 / +10 buttons and the Ω readout (not on the real rheostat: R is set with the ohmmeter, as in the manual) |
| Names | «R1 … R4» printed on the desk under each one (the group's names, not on the device) |
