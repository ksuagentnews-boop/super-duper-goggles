# Variable AC supply (Exp 7): notes (batch 5, item 8)

Model: Leybold 521 35 «Kleinspannungs-Stelltrafo S / variable extra low voltage transformer S». Layout and labels from
`ref/variable-ac-supply-leybold-521-35.jpg`; colours and logo from the lab crop `crops/variable-ac-supply.jpg` (cream case, red rocker,
old L-in-a-circle Leybold logo, thin red line over the logo row). Drawn 450 × 262 (≈ 30 × 17.5 cm front, 1.5 units/mm).

| Element | Look / place |
|---|---|
| Case | cream, carrying strap in the slot on top, black feet |
| Rocker | red, in a black frame, top-left (lit brighter when on) |
| Field 1 (grey) | fuse holder «1.8A»; «12V» fixed ac: two black sockets with ~ |
| Field 2 (grey) | fuse holder «6A»; two black ~ sockets, the variable ac output «0...20V» |
| Knob | silver pointer knob on the field 2 / 3 border, seven dots around it (no numbers) |
| Printed diagram | lines from the ~ sockets to the bridge-rectifier box, on to the dc sockets; «0...20V» on both sides |
| Field 3 (grey) | «−» blue and «+» red sockets: 0 … 20 V dc |
| Bottom | Leybold logo left; «521 35 / KLEINSPANNUNGS-STELLTRAFO S / VARIABLE EXTRA LOW VOLTAGE TRANSFORMER S» right |

Simulator: the variable ~ sockets carry the experiment's leads (acL / acN), 0 … 20 V as printed (was 0 … 24 V); the 12 V ~
sockets work as spare sockets (12 V, 60 Hz); the dc sockets are drawn but not wired (no experiment uses them).
